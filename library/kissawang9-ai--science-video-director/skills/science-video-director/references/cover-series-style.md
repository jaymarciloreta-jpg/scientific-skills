# Knowledge cover series style

Use this as the fixed cover identity for every explainer episode. Transfer the reference's information hierarchy and thumbnail clarity, not its copyrighted characters, exact artwork, or platform UI.

## Fixed visual grammar

1. Near-black charcoal, chalkboard, or dark vertical woodgrain texture fills the background.
2. The top white line names the everyday phenomenon and begins with the truthful explanatory lens, such as `经济学：`, `心理学：`, `社会学：`, `医学：`, or `科技：`.
3. The second red line states the contradiction, threat, or surprising question.
4. One original recurring character appears in the middle with an exaggerated story-specific reaction.
5. One decisive economic prop sits in the character's hand or immediate reach.
6. One large yellow concept phrase anchors the bottom.

The viewer should understand `现象 → 冲突 → 人物遭遇 → 概念答案` without reading the video description.

## Copy limits

- White setup: approximately 6–10 Chinese characters, including the discipline prefix when practical.
- Red contradiction: approximately 7–12 Chinese characters.
- Yellow concept: 2–6 Chinese characters.
- Use exactly three semantic text groups. Do not add subtitles, source notes, English, numbers, hashtags, or decorative fake writing.
- Prefer concrete spoken Chinese. Avoid `深度解析`, `全面解读`, `震惊`, and other empty packaging words.

## Character and prop

- Use the episode's original protagonist, never SpongeBob, Patrick, Squidward, Mr. Krabs, or any other recognizable franchise character.
- Keep one character dominant. A second figure is allowed only when the story conflict cannot be understood without it.
- Use one decisive prop: bill, contract, gold bar, key, shopping bag, inventory box, loan notice, or another concrete mechanism object.
- Expression and pose must communicate the episode's specific pressure rather than a generic smile.

## Color and type

- Background: charcoal black or very dark gray, subtly textured.
- Setup line: warm white.
- Contradiction line: saturated signal red.
- Concept line: strong golden yellow.
- Use bold hand-painted or brush-like Chinese display lettering with a dark shadow/outline for thumbnail legibility.
- Do not imitate a specific creator's exact font treatment or signature marks.

## Ratio layouts

### 3:4 vertical

- Text occupies the upper 30–36%.
- Character and prop occupy the middle 34–42%.
- Yellow concept occupies the bottom 16–22%.
- Keep generous dark breathing space between the red line and character.

### 4:3 horizontal

- Preserve the same top-to-bottom reading order and colors.
- Place the original character slightly left or center; let the prop connect the character to the headline.
- The white and red lines may span the upper width; the yellow concept remains a strong bottom anchor.
- Recompose for the wider canvas. Never crop the 3:4 result.

## Generation and approval

- Generate each final cover directly with ChatCut `gpt-image-2` (`img2`), using the episode's character reference when available.
- Do not use video screenshots or simulated player frames.
- Inspect the exact Chinese text, color order, face, hands, prop, and thumbnail readability.
- Regenerate any cover with extra text, wrong characters, franchise resemblance, a busy scenic background, or a weak yellow concept anchor.
