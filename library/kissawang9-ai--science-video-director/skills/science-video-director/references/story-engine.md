# Story engine

## Non-negotiable sequence

1. **Question hook:** turn the contradiction into a short spoken question, while immediately showing the protagonist and identity card.
2. **Identity and desire:** name the character and what they urgently want.
3. **Decision:** show the choice that looked rational at the time.
4. **Hidden obligation:** introduce the contract, rule, biological constraint, technical dependency, or social incentive that does not move with appearances.
5. **Escalation:** cause at least two visible consequences.
6. **Reversal:** the protagonist discovers that the apparent benefit is the trap.
7. **Concept reveal:** name the mechanism only now.
8. **Mapping:** map at least three earlier story details back to the mechanism.
9. **Resolution:** answer the opening contradiction and give one useful boundary or action.

## Runtime and narrative density

- Runtime is an output of a complete causal story, not a preset quota. Unless the user specifies a target, do not shorten the episode before the contradiction, reversal, mechanism, three mappings, and resolution are understandable.
- A longer script is not permission to repeat. Every beat must add a new event, cost, choice, consequence, or reinterpretation.
- Escalation must deepen meaning, not merely accumulate hardship. Build `personal loss → repeated threat → wider system cost → concept reveal`, or an equivalent topic-specific causal ladder.
- After drafting, remove any line whose deletion does not change the viewer's understanding, emotional investment, or prediction of what happens next.

## Voice

- Default opening: `明明___，为什么反而___？` followed immediately by `我叫___。`
- The opening question belongs to the viewer-facing narrator; after it, hand the story to the protagonist's first-person voice.
- Keep the question to about 22 Chinese characters or fewer so it fits naturally inside five seconds.
- Put a measurable stake or concrete reversal in the question. Do not use greetings, `你知道吗`, topic announcements, definitions, or answer-first openings.
- Do not let the opening narrator continue explaining. Its only job is to create the unresolved question.
- Prefer “我签了、我欠了、我等了、我发现” over “数据显示、从宏观角度看”。
- Allow a narrator only to bridge time or state the final concept.
- Avoid sermon-like conclusions, generic motivation, and fake dialogue that exists only to deliver definitions.

## Visual grammar

- Reuse one environment so viewers track changes instead of relearning the scene.
- Give each important noun a prop and each causal change a visible motion.
- Use character poses for emotion; use arrows and numbers for mechanism; use charts only for scale.
- Make the screen change meaningfully every 2–4 seconds without changing the entire art style.
- Use a fixed bottom subtitle lane with exactly one line.

## Reference-derived profile

The installed reference profile is: provocative contradiction → one character's financial fate → flashback to the original decision → asset/income/obligation diverge → cascading consequences → late formal concept reveal. Transfer this mechanism, not its copyrighted characters, names, voices, setting, or artwork.
