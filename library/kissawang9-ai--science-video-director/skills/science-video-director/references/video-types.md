# Topic-to-story routing

All routes use one protagonist. Choose the person whose concrete decision exposes the mechanism most clearly.

| Topic | Protagonist | Dramatic pressure | Useful props |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economics / finance | borrower, shop owner, worker, saver, policymaker | price, debt, income, liquidity, incentives | contract, bill, bank, price tag, wallet |
| Science mechanism | patient, engineer, field observer, cell/particle personification | failure, uncertainty, scarce time, competing forces | instrument, sample, gauge, diagram-object |
| Medicine / health | fictional patient or clinician | symptom, decision, risk, delayed consequence | report, medicine, timer, body cutaway |
| Technology / AI | user, operator, developer, affected worker | automation, error, trade-off, hidden dependency | interface, queue, model box, permission |
| History | witness, clerk, soldier, merchant, craftsperson, ordinary resident | policy change, scarcity, conflict, rumor, institutional pressure | map, decree, ledger, letter, coin, seal, ticket, goods |
| Product / process | user with a blocked goal | friction, mistake, deadline, state change | interface, checklist, before/after object |
| Myth-busting | believer who acts on the myth | expectation collides with outcome | claim card, test, receipt, result |
| Data story | person represented by one data point | personal outcome differs from average | counter, cohort cards, small chart |

## Duration routing

- 30–45s: hook → choice → reversal → concept → one-line takeaway.
- 45–90s: hook → setup → escalating consequences → reversal → mechanism → takeaway.
- 90–120s: add a flashback, secondary character, or second consequence; keep one protagonist.
- Longer than 120s: use chapters, but preserve the same protagonist and unresolved opening tension.

## Historical route

- Read `historical-editorial-mode.md` before scripting or generating assets.
- Prefer the ordinary person whose decision exposes how the historical change reached daily life. Use a ruler or famous figure only when their personal choice is the actual causal hinge.
- Build the chain as `人物处境 → 证据物件 → 地图或制度变化 → 文件证明 → 回到人物代价`.
- Do not force a 45-second runtime or a fixed nine-shot structure. Let verified evidence and the protagonist's causal story determine the shot count.
- Do not use Seedance or other image-to-video generation. Produce still/keyframe assets and animate separated layers in ChatCut.
